The sandbox shows its demos as a grid of cards with live thumbnails, in a native stack instead of a drawer, in a Theme section (token demos that `loom init` copies with the theme) and a Components section, searched by title from the native header; demos drop their `keywords`, and the manifest's `demo` becomes a `demos` list; on iOS 18+ a card zooms into its demo.
